Ideal ranges

“Slightly out” = within margin
TestLowHighMargin

Total chlorine uses the combined-chlorine range: TC is in range when TC − FC is within the CC high value. Margin: a value past the range by no more than the margin is “slightly out” (amber, !); further is “well out” (red, !!).

Formulas

Combined chlorine = TC − FC.
LSI = pH − pHs, where pHs = (9.3 + A + B) − (C + D); A = (log₁₀ TDS − 1) / 10 with TDS taken as the salt reading (1,000 ppm if none); B = −13.12 · log₁₀(°C + 273) + 34.55; C = log₁₀(calcium hardness) − 0.4; D = log₁₀(TA − CYA / 3). LSI is shown only for tests with a water temperature.
